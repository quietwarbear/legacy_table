# Legacy Table — "Your recipes are yours" copy pack

Handoff for the developer, the website, and the App Store listing. One promise, said the same way everywhere. Ship in the order listed; the in-app card is the priority.

**The promise (canonical wording, do not paraphrase):**

> Your recipes are yours. We never sell them, share them, or use them without you. You can download or delete everything, any time.

**Short form (buttons, footers, captions):** Your recipes stay yours.

---

## 1. In-app: first "Share a Recipe" tap

Show once, before the recipe form opens. Dismiss button stores a flag so it never shows again. Also reachable later from Settings → "Who owns my recipes?"

**Title:** Before you share

**Body:**
Your recipes are yours. We never sell them, share them, or use them without you. Only the family members you invite can see what you save. You can download or delete everything, any time.

**Button:** Got it

---

## 2. In-app: footer line on Family Cookbook and Upgrade screens

Small text under the primary content, links to the Settings page in section 1.

Your recipes stay yours. Upgrading unlocks family features, never access to your own recipes.

---

## 3. In-app: Settings page — "Who owns my recipes?"

**You do.**
Every recipe, photo, voice note and story you add to Legacy Table belongs to you and your family. We don't sell recipes. We don't license them to anyone. We don't use them to train anything or to make our own cookbooks.

**Who can see them?**
Only the people you invite to your family cookbook. Nothing you save is public unless you choose to share it yourself.

**Can I take them with me?**
Yes. Download your whole cookbook from Settings at any time. Delete your account and everything goes with it.

**What does paying for?**
A free account already stores your recipes. Upgrading adds family features: more members, shared cookbooks, and celebration planning. It never changes who owns what you've saved.

## 4. Website: legacytable.app

**Homepage — one line under the hero, before any feature or pricing content:**
Your recipes stay yours. We never sell or share them.

**Pricing page — section above the plans, headed "Who owns my recipes?":**
You do. Free or paid, every recipe you save belongs to you and the family you invite. We never sell recipes, license them, or use them for anything you didn't choose. Paying unlocks family features, never access to your own food. Download or delete everything, any time.

**Footer link:** Who owns my recipes? → links to the pricing-page section or the full ownership page in the terms.

## 5. App Store listing

**Subtitle (30 characters max, App Store field):**
Your recipes stay yours

If the subtitle is already in use for positioning, keep it and put the line as the second sentence of the description instead.

**Description — insert as the second paragraph, after the opening pitch:**
Your recipes are yours. Legacy Table never sells, shares, or reuses what your family saves. Create a free account to start; upgrading unlocks family features, never access to your own recipes. Download or delete everything, any time.

**Keywords to keep clear of:** anything implying a marketplace, "sell," "monetize," "publish."

---

## 6. Terms of Service — plain-English ownership clause

Add near the top of the terms, ahead of the legal definitions, so it is the first thing a reader sees. Have the lawyer confirm the legal version below it matches.

**Your content belongs to you.**
You own everything you add to Legacy Table: recipes, photos, audio, stories, and notes. We do not sell it, license it to third parties, or use it to build products of our own. We store and display it only so the app works for you and the family members you invite. You can export or delete your content at any time, and deleting your account deletes it from our systems.
