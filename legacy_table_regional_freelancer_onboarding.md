## What Legacy Table is

Legacy Table is a private, invite-only family app for preserving recipes, photos, voice notes, and food traditions across generations. It was built by **Hodari and Shylah Touré** after losing a grandmother's gumbo recipe to a hospital stay no one expected. The product is a love letter to families that have watched a dish disappear with a person — and a tool for the ones that don't have to.

- **Platforms:** iOS and Android (live on both stores)
- **Audience:** families — multi-generational, food-loving, culturally rooted
- **Pricing:** free tier + paid subscription tiers (managed in-app via App Store / Google Play). Public pricing page: legacytable.app/pricing
- **Brand site:** legacytable.app
- **Founder voice:** warm, intentional, intergenerational, never preachy

## How to post (Buffer workflow)

The setup is designed so you never need direct passwords to Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, or Facebook. Buffer is your single login.

### Your daily flow

1. Log into Buffer at **publish.buffer.com** with the credentials in your Bitwarden vault.
2. You'll see Legacy Table's 4 channels in the left sidebar (IG, TikTok, LinkedIn, FB).
3. Click **+ New Post**, pick the channel(s), write your caption, drop the appropriate **bit.ly** link in the caption, attach media, schedule.
4. Done — Buffer publishes at the scheduled time.

### Approval workflow (may be enabled for your region)

If Shy has turned on approval for your region, your scheduled posts queue up under **Pending Approval** until Shy reviews and clicks Approve. Build in a 24–48 hour lead time so approvals don't bottleneck your schedule.

### Posting cadence — minimum expectations

| Channel | Minimum posts/week | Recommended posts/week |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram (feed + Stories) | 3 feed + daily Stories | 4–5 feed + daily Stories |
| TikTok | 3 | 5–7 |
| LinkedIn | 2 | 3 |
| Facebook | 2 | 3 (often cross-post from IG) |

Pick the cadence within those ranges that fits your region's audience — e.g., evenings in [YOUR TIMEZONE] often outperform mornings for food content. Use Buffer's analytics tab to see what's working week over week.

## Brand voice cheat sheet

**Tone:** warm, intentional, intergenerational, lightly nostalgic, occasionally poetic. Never preachy, never sales-y.

**Do**
- Lead with stories ("My grandmother used to add bay leaves at the end…")
- Center elders and the act of passing down
- Honor cultural specificity — recipes are from somewhere, made by someone, for someone
- Use the founders' names (Hodari and Shylah Touré) when relevant
- Talk about *families* as the unit, not individuals
- Photograph hands, kitchens, ingredients in process — not finished plated meals on white linen

**Don't**
- Politics, controversial social topics, or anything polarizing
- Diet talk, weight talk, "guilt-free" / "clean eating" framing
- Influencer-style "swipe up for the recipe!!" energy
- Anything that mocks or condescends about traditional cooking
- AI-generated food photos (we want the real thing)
- Comparing Legacy Table to other apps by name — talk about what makes us different, not what they're not

**Hashtags (always include 3–5 per post)**

Core: `#LegacyTable` `#FamilyRecipes` `#FoodHeritage` `#RecipesWithStories` `#PreserveTheRecipe`

Region-specific: layer in 2–3 hashtags that work in [YOUR REGION]'s language and cultural context. Coordinate with Shy if unsure.

## Content themes that work

These have tested well historically — use them as a starting point, not a cage:

- **"The recipe that almost got lost"** — short stories about saving a family dish, ideally featuring real users (with permission)
- **Voice note Sundays** — a 30–60 second clip of a grandmother explaining a step, with the recipe card overlay
- **Cooking with elders** — short reels of two generations cooking together
- **Holiday tradition deep-dives** — feature the recipe + the family memory behind it, timed to local holidays in your region
- **Heritage festival coverage** — when there's a regional food festival, send a short recap with the link
- **Founder content** — Hodari and Shylah doing a recipe live, or short interviews. Coordinate with Shy to schedule.
