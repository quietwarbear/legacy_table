## 2.3.9 (build 265 Android / 127+ iOS)

This update focuses on stability. We fixed several crashes that could happen
while browsing recipes, saving a recipe, scanning or recording, and editing
your profile. Thanks for using Legacy Table — keep the recipes coming!

_Under the hood: 22 unmounted-`setState` crash guards (#60) and the iOS
clipboard launch-hang fix (#61)._
